# Liquify Edit and Apply

Learn how to edit your curves with liquify and apply the changes.
## Edit and Distort
Liquify allows you to distort and reshape selected curves or drawings. You can create
unique forms by distorting specific areas or make meticulous adjustments across the entire
piece.
## Press and Drag to Liquify

Press and drag with your pen to liquify your curves or drawings. The degree of distortion and the
affected area depend on the size, range, and strength settings in the Liquify panel.
Learn more about the Liquify Panel.
## Push
Distorts naturally, as if pushing or pulling with a finger. Useful in most situations.

## Pinch
Distorts sharply and precisely, as if pinching. Useful for extending or shrinking specific curves.

## Comb
Gently smooths and aligns as if combing. Ideal for straightening wavy curves. Works best when
used with a rubbing motion.
## Compare and Apply
Comparing before and after changes is crucial to understanding the overall impact. Always
compare before applying liquify.

## Undo All
Tap the 'Undo All' icon on the left of the bottom context menu to revert to the state before liquify.
To undo step by step, use the history panel.

## Compare
Tap and hold 'Compare' in the center of the bottom context menu to view the curves before
liquify.

## Apply
Tap the checkbox icon on the right of the bottom context menu to apply all liquify changes.
