# Extensions Sequence

Learn how to save and play back camera shots to create simple animations.
## Edit Sequence
Find and save the shots and angles you want. Save and arrange shots in a sequence. This
can also be useful for saving specific scenes.
## Tap Sequence in System Menu

Tap Sequence in the system menu at the top right of the screen. When using Sequence, other
interfaces will be hidden. Tap Back at the top right of the screen to return. Even if you close the
sequence, the saved shots will remain.
## Add Shot
Tap the + icon in the Sequence tab to add a shot. The shot will capture the current view and
angle. Tapping an added shot will instantly switch to that view. Shots are always added in the next
order relative to the active shot.

## Tap and Hold to Select
Tap and hold on a shot with your finger or pen to select it. You can select multiple shots by
tapping another shot while one is already selected. Tap a selected shot again to deselect it.

## Change Sequence Order
With a sequence selected, tap the directional icons on either side to change the sequence order.

## Delete Shot
Select a shot and tap the trash can icon on the right of the sequence to delete it. Note that
deleted shots cannot be recovered.
## Play Sequence
Play back and view your shots. Create presentations to show others or make simple
animations.

## Play and Pause
After adding one or more shots, tap Play in the sequence to switch to playback controls. Press the
play button in the center of the screen to play the sequence. Press it again to pause. Tap the
arrows on either side of the play button to switch between shots.

## Tap and Slide Timeline
Tap and slide the timeline at the bottom of the sequence to manually move through the timeline.

## Change Playback Speed
Tap the number on the left of the sequence to change the playback speed. You can play at 0.5x,
1x, or 2x speed.

## Change Playback Modes
Tap the number on the right of the sequence to change the playback mode. You can switch
between play once, loop, and swing modes.
## Options
Use these options for more precise and detailed sequence settings.

## Tap Sequence Options
Tap the Sequence Options on the right of the sequence to toggle various options.
## UI Always On
Tap 'UI Always On' to keep the sequence interface visible even during playback.
## Thirds Grid
Tap 'Thirds Grid' to overlay a 3x3 grid on your camera. This is especially useful for composing your
shot.
## Camera Info

Tap 'Camera Info' to check the current location, rotation, and lens field of view of the camera. The
location and rotation are displayed in X, Y, and Z axes, representing the current distance and
angles.
