# Assistance Stable Strokes

Learn how to adjust stroke stabilization.
## Stable Stroke
Use Stable Stroke to switch between freeform and clean lines.
Enable Stable Stroke from the System Menu. Adjust the slider left or right to control the
smoothing level.

## Preview
Test it while you draw.
Draw directly in the Stable Stroke panel to see how much the line is smoothed in real-time.

## Adjusting Stable Stroke from the Squeeze Menu
If you're using the Squeeze Menu, you can quickly adjust your stroke stabilization. Unfold the
Squeeze Menu, then tap Stable Stroke to switch it on or off, or tap and slide up or down to adjust
its intensity.
