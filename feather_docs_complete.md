![intro](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FWelcome%20to%20the%20world%20of%203D%20drawing..ce05dace.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Welcome to the world of 3D drawing.[](#welcome-to-the-world-of-3d-drawing)

Feather lets you draw 3D lines using natural pen and touch input. You can draw a line, view it from different perspectives, and add more lines to enrich your imagination without hassle. Developed by creative professionals, Feather utilizes "3D sketching" technology that integrates design, computer graphics, and human-computer interaction.

This comprehensive documentation covers all of Feather's interfaces, gestures, and features. It also provides resources to help you get the most out of Feather, including essential 3D drawing techniques, extensions, and Blender add-ons.

Whatever you can imagine, let's bring it to life in 3D with Feather.

**The note page layout is divided into three main parts. Explore each section to see what features they offer.**

![notelayout](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FLayout.a7510a91.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

#### 1. Home[](#1-home)

Returns to the home screen.

#### 2. Open System Menu[](#2-open-system-menu)

Opens the system menu list.

### Tool Menu (top right)[](#tool-menu-top-right)

#### 3. Draw and Draw Shape[](#3-draw-and-draw-shape)

Draws 3D guides or curves. You can draw freely or draw straight lines or shapes. Switches with each tap.

#### 4. Erase and Vacuum[](#4-erase-and-vacuum)

Erases with the brush. You can erase partially or remove an entire curve at once. Switches with each tap.

Selects curves or 3D guides.

Turns the mirror on or off, allowing you to draw symmetrically.

Opens the clipboard where you can add image references.

Opens the stage panel where you can access the group tab, resource tab, and environment tab.

### Bottom Context Menu (bottom center)[](#bottom-context-menu-bottom-center)

#### 11. 3D Guide Tap[](#11-3d-guide-tap)

Provides various menus for the 3D guide based on the context. From left to right, you can use Draw 3D Guide, Loft, and Primitives.

# Navigation

Move around the canvas and reach your desired location with ease.

## Basic Navigation[](#basic-navigation)

**Rotate your canvas, zoom, pan and change the viewing angle at your fingertips.**

![swipetorotate](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FSwipe%20to%20Rotate.119a9118.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Swipe to Rotate[](#swipe-to-rotate)

**Start your work by rotating the canvas like a turntable.**

Place your finger on the canvas and swipe to rotate.

![doubletaptoperfectview](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FDouble%20Tap%20to%20Perfect%20View.1f25e769.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Double Tap to Perfect View[](#double-tap-to-perfect-view)

**Align your view precisely for detailed drawing.**

Swipe to rotate the canvas to your desired angle, then double-tap with one finger to snap to a standard orthographic view like Left, Right, Front, Back, Top and Bottom.

![pinchtozoom](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FPinch%20to%20Zoom.d576003c.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Pinch to Zoom[](#pinch-to-zoom)

**Use a pinch to zoom in and out of your 3D artwork to move form fine details to the big picture.**

Place your fingers on the canvas and pinch your fingers together to zoom out. Pinch your fingers apart to zoom in.

![pinchswipetopan](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FPinch-Swipe%20to%20Pan.a1aa9457.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Pinch-Swipe to Pan[](#pinch-swipe-to-pan)

**Swipe to pan and freely navigate your canvas. Quickly move to any position you want.**

Place two fingers on the canvas and swipe them together to pan.

## Advanced Navigation[](#advanced-navigation)

**Adjust the field of view (FOV) to navigate around your chosen focus. And set the orbit point, allowing precise control and effortless movement across the canvas.**

![treefingerdoubletap](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FThree-Finger%20Double%20Tap%20to%20Switch%20Views.ef0469d6.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Three-Finger Double Tap to Switch Views[](#three-finger-double-tap-to-switch-views)

**Switch between perspective and orthographic views quickly.**

Double-tap with three fingers from your desired angle to switch between perspective and orthographic views.

![treefingerswipe](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FThree-Finger%20Swipe%20to%20Change%20FOV.4e9a4155.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Three-Finger Swipe to Change FOV[](#three-finger-swipe-to-change-fov)

**Adjust the filed of view (FOV) to find your perfect shots.**

Place your three fingers on the canvas and swipe up to increase the field of view (FOV) or swipe down to reduce it. The FOV can be adjusted from 10mm to 500mm.

![orbitpoint](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Hold%20to%20Set%20Orbit%20Point-1.581e7b7c.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Hold to Set Orbit Point[](#tap-and-hold-to-set-orbit-point)

**Use the pin orbit point to change the navigation center.**

Tap and hold on a curve or grid to pin the orbit point. Tap and hold in empty space to unpin the orbit point. If the orbit point is not pinned, tap and hold in empty space to reset the view.

The orbit point also functions as a focus point for Depth of Field (DOF). Learn more about [Depth of Field](https://support.feather.art/docs/stagepanel/environmenttab#depth-of-field).

### Oribit Point Options[](#oribit-point-options)

**Toggle the Orbit Point on or off.**

![orbitpointoptions](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Hold%20to%20Set%20Orbit%20Point.51d53fe8.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

Enable the Pin orbit point option to pin the orbit point. Use the Show orbit point option to toggle the visibility of the orbit point on or off.

# Apple Pencil

Customize Apple Pencil gestures and explore various ways to use it.

## Gestures[](#gestures)

**Navigate with your fingers, draw with your pen. Gestures are the finishing touch, letting you create faster and more efficiently than ever.**

![gesturecustom](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FGesture%20Customization.fd9d9c9d.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Gesture Customization[](#gesture-customization)

**Feather supports various gestures depending on your Apple Pencil model. Double Tap, and even the Apple Pencil Pro's Squeeze gesture, are fully supported. Best of all, they're completely customizable.**

Enter the Control Menu from the System Menu. Tap on 'Apple Pencil' to set up various Apple Pencil options.

![squeeze](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FSqueeze.7e4d69e4.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Squeeze[](#squeeze)

**Gently squeeze the lower part of your Pencil. Supported only by Apple Pencil Pro.**

Enter Squeeze from the Apple Pencil Menu. You can assign various functions to this gesture. By default, it's set to quickly access context-sensitive tools through the [Squeeze Menu](https://support.feather.art/docs/interfaceandgestures/squeezemenu).

![doubletap](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FDouble%20Tap.ffa49187.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Double Tap[](#double-tap)

**Lightly double-tap your Pencil. Supported by Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil 2nd Gen.**

Enter Double Tap from the Apple Pencil Menu. Like Squeeze, you can assign various functions to this gesture. By default, it's set to quickly toggle between Draw and Erase modes, but you can also assign it to the Squeeze Menu or Injector.

Learn more about [Injector](https://support.feather.art/docs/brushes/interface#injector).

![toggledrawerase](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FToggle%20Draw%20or%20Erase.88389c9e.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Toggle Draw or Erase[](#toggle-draw-or-erase)

Quickly switch between Draw and Erase modes. You can activate this feature using Squeeze or Double Tap gestures. When using the Select tool, it toggles between Select and Deselect.

![squeezeswitch](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FSqueeze%20Switch.7e4710d3.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Squeeze-drag to Switch[](#squeeze-drag-to-switch)

**An incredibly efficient gesture to quickly switch tools. Squeeze your pencil while placing it on the canvas to temporarily change tools until the pen is lifted.**

Tap a tool from the Tool Menu, then squeeze and drag your Pencil to switch freely between tools. For example, squeezing while "Draw" is active switches to "Draw Shape," and squeezing while "Erase" is active switches to "Vacuum." It works both ways, and similarly switch between "Select" and "Deselect."

Learn more about [Draw and Erase](https://support.feather.art/docs/brushes/drawanderase).

## Accessibility[](#accessibility)

**Adjust various accesibility options for Pencil or touch interactions.**

![showcursor](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FShow%20Cursor.e250e313.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Show Cursor[](#show-cursor)

Enter Show Cursor from the Apple Pencil Menu. Displays a cursor indicating your current tool and position while drawing, erasing, selecting, or deselecting. On iPads supporting Hover, you can preview the size and color of the brush on the 3D guide.

![toggletouch](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FFinger-Pen.041e872d.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Finger-Pen[](#finger-pen)

Tap the system menu at the top left of the screen. Tap the rightmost list, then toggle Finger-Pen at the bottom. Tap again to turn off Finger-Pen.

![taptouch](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20to%20Activate%20Draw%20with%20Finger.50a40519.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap to Activate Finger-Pen[](#tap-to-activate-finger-pen)

Tap Finger-Pen at the bottom of the screen to activate it, indicated by a black highlight. In this state, the next touch will be recognized as a pen press or draw action.

![drawtouch](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FDraw%20with%20Your%20Finger.6534f08c.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Draw with Your Finger[](#draw-with-your-finger)

With Finger-Pen activated, drag your finger to draw. Use Finger-Pen to create 3D Guides or draw on existing 3D Guides, as well as using the Eraser and Select tools. Tap Finger-Pen again to deactivate it and return to navigation.

**Tips** 
When Finger-Pen is enabled, finger-based navigation is locked. It can be used like a screen lock when you want to focus on drawing or editing without accidentally moving the screen.

![tapandholdtouch](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Hold%20Draw%20with%20Finger%20to%20Draw.50b2b8aa.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Hold Finger-Pen to Draw[](#tap-and-hold-finger-pen-to-draw)

Tap and hold Finger-Pen, then use another finger to touch or drag. This allows you to quickly switch between drawing and navigating without toggling Finger-Pen on and off.

![customtouch](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FCustomize.5fbd0cdf.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Customize[](#customize)

Tap and hold the Finger-Pen icon and drag to move it to a preferred position on the screen.

# Squeeze Menu

## Default Menu[](#default-menu)

**The default Squeeze Menu available across all contexts.**

![placeholder](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FUnfold%20Squeeze%20Menu.b28b94a3.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Unfold Squeeze Menu[](#unfold-squeeze-menu)

**A magical palette that appears wherever you are.**

Assign the Squeeze gesture from Apple Pencil settings in the Control Menu, then squeeze or double-tap your Pencil to unfold the Squeeze Menu. It unfolds in the area you last interacted with. If your device supports hover, it appears in the screen area you're hovering over.

### Find Group[](#find-group)

Tap the magnifying glass icon next to Undo  Redo to use Find Group. Hover or place your Pencil over a curve to see the group's name. Lift your Pencil or press while hovering to activate the selected group.

Learn more about [Group Tab](https://support.feather.art/docs/stagepanel/grouptab).

## Contextual Menu[](#contextual-menu)

**A smart Squeeze Menu that automatically appears based on your current action, significantly boosting speed and efficiency.**

Tap the plus icon from the Squeeze Menu to add a new group, automatically activating it. Available when "Draw" is active from the Tool Menu.

Learn more about [Add New Group](https://support.feather.art/docs/stagepanel/grouptab#add-new-group).

![placeholder](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FQuick%20Brush%20Panel.28cb11d9.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

Adjust Stable Stroke intensity, toggle Pressure Sensitivity, or use the Injector. Available when "Draw" is active from the Tool Menu.

Learn more about [Brush Panel](https://support.feather.art/docs/brushes/interface#brush-panel).

![placeholder](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FRecall%20Recent%20Guide.1207a58a.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Recall Recent Guide[](#recall-recent-guide)

Reload the most recently closed 3D guide. Available when "Draw" is active from the Tool Menu and a recent guide was closed.

Learn more about [3D Guide](https://support.feather.art/docs/3dguide).

![placeholder](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FSelect%20All.964b7a14.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Select All[](#select-all)

Select all currently visible groups. Hidden groups remain deselected. Available when "Select" is active from the Tool Menu.

Learn more about [Selection](https://support.feather.art/docs/selection).

![placeholder](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FStamp.b48f35f6.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Stamp[](#stamp)

Quickly duplicate and move selected curves or groups. Available when "Select" is active from the Tool Menu, with curves selected.

![placeholder](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FBarrel%20Roll%20During%20Stamp.8091f3cf.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

After tapping Stamp, drag your Pencil to duplicate. While duplicating, twist a Barrel Roll-enabled Pencil left or right to rotate curves as you duplicate. Tap "Done" or select another tool to finish stamping.

#### 3. Close Button[](#3-close-button)

Tap the Close Button to remove the 3D Guide. This action can be undone. Also, tap and slide sideways the close button to adjust the opacity. Tap and slide upward the clost button to save the 3D Guide.

#### 4. Resource Tab[](#4-resource-tab)

The 3D Guide is managed in the Resource Tab of the [Stage Panel](https://support.feather.art/docs/stagepanel/interface).

![adjustguide](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Slide%20Sideways%20to%20Adjust%20Opacity.544b1a5d.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

**Continue creating without being hindered by 3D Guide.**

Tap the close button and then slide left or right to adjust the opacity of 3D Guide. Sliding left decreases the opacity, and sliding right increases it. The opacity of 3D Guide can be reduced to 0%, but it cannot be made completely opaque.

![saveguide](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Slide%20Upward%20to%20Save%203D%20Guide.8a9ebc2f.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Draw with Pen[](#draw-with-pen)

**Create 3D Guide as if you're drawing with a pen.**

Tap the Draw icon and set the bottom context menu to Draw. Then, use your pen to draw on the screen, and the 3D Guide will be created. The 3D Guide is generated perpendicular to your viewing angle and varies based on your [Field of View(FOV)](https://support.feather.art/docs/interfaceandgestures/navigation#three-finger-swipe-to-change-fov).

**Bend an existing 3D Guide to create more organic shapes, revolve, or make a tube.**

While a 3D Guide is active, tap the Bend icon below the Draw icon. Then, use your pen to draw on the screen, and the 3D Guide will bend along the drawn line.

The bending starts from the orange line, which is the starting point of the 3D Guide. You can repeat the Bend 3D Guide process multiple times.

### Erase[](#erase)

Tap the Eraser in the tool menu. Tap or drag with your pen to erase parts of the curve.

**Tips** 
The Eraser removes points from the center of the curve, not the surrounding geometry. If you’ve drawn with a broad brush and the Eraser doesn’t touch the center, it may appear as though it’s not erasing.

![vaccum](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FVacuum.5e8933e2.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Vaccum[](#vaccum)

**Define areas you don’t want to erase. Another way to use 3D guides.**

**The 2D Joystick moves, rotates, and scales objects based on the view direction. It's very intuitive because it transforms as it appears.**

![tapmove](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Drag%20Stick%20to%20Move.424173a4.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Drag Stick to Move[](#tap-and-drag-stick-to-move)

Tap and hold the center circle of the 2D Joystick to turn it black. Drag to move the selected curve or resource in that direction. The stick can move outside the joystick layout. When you release, the stick returns to its original position.

![tapscale](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Drag%20Scale%20Handle.145fc42b.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Drag Scale Handle[](#tap-and-drag-scale-handle)

Tap and drag the scale handles located above and to the left of the stick to scale the selected curve or resource. Handles allow height scaling, width scaling, or free scaling. The scaling reference point is the center of the screen, marked with a crosshair.

![taprotate](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Spin%20Rotate%20Handle.e7d61fa4.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Spin Rotate Handle[](#tap-and-spin-rotate-handle)

Tap and spin the rotate handle on the right of the stick to rotate the selected curve or resource. The rotation center point is the center of the screen, marked with a crosshair.

## Lock 2D Joystick[](#lock-2d-joystick)

**Locking the joystick restricts movement and rotation values. It's useful for precise transformations.**

![lockmove](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Drag%20Stick%20to%20Move%20in%204%20Directions.3b19e48b.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Drag Stick to Move in 4 Directions[](#tap-and-drag-stick-to-move-in-4-directions)

When the 2D Joystick is locked, tapping and dragging the stick only allows movement up, down, left, and right.

![lockscale](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Drag%20Scale%20Handle%20for%20Uniform%20Scaling.83780c16.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Drag Scale Handle for Uniform Scaling[](#tap-and-drag-scale-handle-for-uniform-scaling)

The locked 2D Joystick has a single scale handle. Tap and drag it to uniformly scale the selected curve or resource. The scaling reference point is the center of
the screen.

![lockrotate](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Spin%20Rotate%20Handle%20for%2015-Degree%20Increments.831059cb.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Spin Rotate Handle for 15-Degree Increments[](#tap-and-spin-rotate-handle-for-15-degree-increments)

Tap and spin the rotate handle on the locked 2D Joystick to rotate the selected curve or resource in 15-degree increments. It's useful for rotating to specific angles like 90 or 180 degrees. The rotation center point is the center of the screen.

**The 3D Joystick moves or rotates objects based on the global XYZ axes. The global axes appear differently depending on the view direction, and so does the 3D Joystick.**

Tap and spin the red, green, or blue arc of the 3D Joystick to rotate along the respective axis. Red is for the global X axis, green for the Y axis, and blue for the Z axis. The rotation center point is the invisible center of the selected object or resource.

![freerotate](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FFree%20Rotate.364756c3.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Free Rotate[](#free-rotate)

Tap and drag the center sphere of the 3D Joystick for free rotation. You can rotate the selected curve or resource like a trackball, and the rotation center point is the invisible center of the selected object.

![3djoyperfectview](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FIn%20Perfect%20Views.405d1103.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### In Perfect Views[](#in-perfect-views)

**The 3D Joystick is also useful for understanding the view direction.**

In perfect views like the front view, side view, or top view, you can only use two of the three cones on the 3D Joystick. The shape of the arcs also changes based on the view. This is a natural occurrence due to the view direction, so do not be confused.

# Sequence

Learn how to save and play back camera shots to create simple animations.

## Edit Sequence[](#edit-sequence)

**Find and save the shots and angles you want. Save and arrange shots in a sequence. This can also be useful for saving specific scenes.**

![editsequence](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20Sequence%20in%20System%20Menu.6cdab7e9.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap Sequence in System Menu[](#tap-sequence-in-system-menu)

Tap Sequence in the system menu at the top right of the screen. When using Sequence, other interfaces will be hidden. Tap Back at the top right of the screen to return. Even if you close the sequence, the saved shots will remain.

![addshot](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FAdd%20Shot.56dc269b.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Add Shot[](#add-shot)

Tap the + icon in the Sequence tab to add a shot. The shot will capture the current view and angle. Tapping an added shot will instantly switch to that view. Shots are always added in the next order relative to the active shot.

![taphold](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Hold%20to%20Select.27aff54d.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Hold to Select[](#tap-and-hold-to-select)

Tap and hold on a shot with your finger or pen to select it. You can select multiple shots by tapping another shot while one is already selected. Tap a selected shot again to deselect it.

![changesequence](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FChange%20Sequence%20Order.0d396fcf.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Change Sequence Order[](#change-sequence-order)

With a sequence selected, tap the directional icons on either side to change the sequence order.

![deleteshot](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FDelete%20Shot.e045ee83.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Delete Shot[](#delete-shot)

Select a shot and tap the trash can icon on the right of the sequence to delete it. Note that deleted shots cannot be recovered.

## Play Sequence[](#play-sequence)

**Play back and view your shots. Create presentations to show others or make simple animations.**

![playsequence](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FPlay%20and%20Pause.d15c09be.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Play and Pause[](#play-and-pause)

After adding one or more shots, tap Play in the sequence to switch to playback controls. Press the play button in the center of the screen to play the sequence. Press it again to pause. Tap the arrows on either side of the play button to switch between shots.

![slidetimeline](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20and%20Slide%20Timeline.f71a3812.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap and Slide Timeline[](#tap-and-slide-timeline)

Tap and slide the timeline at the bottom of the sequence to manually move through the timeline.

![changespeed](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FChange%20Playback%20Speed.0811cfc4.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Change Playback Speed[](#change-playback-speed)

Tap the number on the left of the sequence to change the playback speed. You can play at 0.5x, 1x, or 2x speed.

![changeplaybackmode](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FChange%20Playback%20Modes.05e78b03.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Change Playback Modes[](#change-playback-modes)

Tap the number on the right of the sequence to change the playback mode. You can switch between play once, loop, and swing modes.

## Options[](#options)

**Use these options for more precise and detailed sequence settings.**

![sequenceoptions](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FSequence%20Options.de8a4de2.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap Sequence Options[](#tap-sequence-options)

Tap the Sequence Options on the right of the sequence to toggle various options.

### UI Always On[](#ui-always-on)

Tap 'UI Always On' to keep the sequence interface visible even during playback.

### Thirds Grid[](#thirds-grid)

Tap 'Thirds Grid' to overlay a 3x3 grid on your camera. This is especially useful for composing your shot.

### Camera Info[](#camera-info)

Tap 'Camera Info' to check the current location, rotation, and lens field of view of the camera. The location and rotation are displayed in X, Y, and Z axes, representing the current distance and angles.

# Export

Learn how to export your sketches in different formats.

## Export Tab[](#export-tab)

**Quickly export your sketches to various platforms. Supports image, video, and 3D model exports.**

![exporttab](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20Export%20in%20System%20Menu.b4df814f.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap Export in System Menu[](#tap-export-in-system-menu)

Tap export in the system menu at the top right of the screen to open the Export Tab.

![quickexport](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FQuick%20Export.2385c45d.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Quick Export[](#quick-export)

Quickly export using preset values. You can quick export in PNG, GIF, and GLTF formats. GIFs are rendered as 360-degree turntable animations centered on the current orbit point.

## Format[](#format)

**Export in more formats with detailed settings.**

![imageexport](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FImage%20Export.a6d38843.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Image Export[](#image-export)

Export as PNG or JPG. You can select the aspect ratio and size of the image. If you export as PNG, a transparent background option is available. When the transparent background is toggled, effects are not applied.

Learn more about [Effects](https://support.feather.art/docs/stagepanel/environmenttab#effects).

![videoexport](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FVideo%20Export.71b53e1e.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Video Export[](#video-export)

Export as MP4 or GIF. When exporting as MP4, you can adjust the bitrate and frame rate.

![mp4export](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FMP4%20Export.7eeb5a97.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### MP4 Export[](#mp4-export)

Tap 'Start Recording' and then tap the record button located at the bottom center of the screen. During recording, you can navigate freely and pause or cancel at any time. Tap the record button once again to stop the recording.

![videopresets](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FPreset.77d93e50.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Preset[](#preset)

Tap the preset list to change the video preset. You can choose from Sequence, 360 Rotation, and Timelapse.

### Sequence[](#sequence)

Exports the sequence as an MP4. A sequence must be set up before exporting.

Learn more about [Sequences](https://support.feather.art/docs/extensions/sequence).

### 360 Rotation[](#360-rotation)

Exports a 360-degree turntable animation. It follows the current orbit point.

Learn more about [Orbit Points](https://support.feather.art/docs/interfaceandgestures/navigation#oribit-point-options).

### Timelapse[](#timelapse)

Exports a timelapse animation. The timelapse follows the group order, and you can navigate freely during recording.

![gifexport](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FGIF%20Export.4da12865.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### GIF Export[](#gif-export)

GIF Export via Video Export exports the 360 turntable animation just like a Quick Export centered on the current orbit point.

![3dmodelexport](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2F3D%20Model%20Export.5879fc7d.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### 3D Model Export[](#3d-model-export)

Export as OBJ or GLTF format. Only groups with visibility turned ON will be exported. If you want to export only specific parts of your artwork or sketch, hide the other groups. The GLTF format exports with baked vertex painting. For editing the exported OBJ or GLTF models in Blender, refer to [Blender Add-on](https://support.feather.art/docs/blenderaddon/howtoinstall).

# Publish to Gallery

Learn how to share your work on the Gallery.

## Share Your Note[](#share-your-note)

**Prepare the best moments of your note and share them in the Gallery. Great for feedback, building your portfolio, or simply showing off.**

![tappublishtogalleryinsystemmenu](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FTap%20Publish%20to%20Gallery%20in%20System%20Menu.02b25bb9.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Tap Publish to Gallery in System Menu[](#tap-publish-to-gallery-in-system-menu)

Tap Publish to Gallery in the system menu at the top right of the screen. When publishing, all menus will be hidden, and your note will automatically save.

**Note** 
Effects such as Glow are not supported in the Gallery. Preview carefully before publishing.

![verifypublishinformation](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FVerify%20Publish%20Information.347c6b4b.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Verify Publish Information[](#verify-publish-information)

Check the profile you're publishing from. To publish to the Gallery, you need to sign in to Feather. If you're not signed in, set up your profile from the home screen settings under [Profile Options](https://support.feather.art/docs/home/settings#profile-options).

![editdescription](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FEdit%20Description.1bab6709.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Edit Description[](#edit-description)

Edit your note's title and description. You can add tags to help others discover your note more easily in the Gallery. Toggle Work-in-Progress to mark your note as currently being worked on. Toggle Downloadable to allow others to download your note, excluding any [Imported Resources](https://support.feather.art/docs/stagepanel/resourcetab#import-image).

![editsequence](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FEdit%20Sequence.73359b0c.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Edit Sequence[](#edit-sequence)

Toggle Include Sequence and tap Edit to add or remove shots from the sequence. When finished, tap Done at the top left of the screen.

Learn more about [Sequence](https://support.feather.art/docs/extensions/sequence).

![chooselicense](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FChoose%20License.af350eee.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Choose License[](#choose-license)

Select a Creative Commons license. If you don't select any license, it defaults to All Rights Reserved. Find descriptions of each license at [Creative Commons (opens in a new tab)](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/).

![preview](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FPreview.f780f8ef.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Preview[](#preview)

Check how your note will appear in the Gallery with the final preview. Tap Back to return to previous steps. Tap Capture Thumbnail to set the thumbnail image for your note. When everything is ready, tap Publish.

![publishconfirmation](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FPublish%20Confirmation.499a1373.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Publish Confirmation[](#publish-confirmation)

Once your note is successfully published, you'll see a confirmation message at the top of the screen. Tap the hyperlink in the message to go directly to your published note.

## Capture Thumbnail[](#capture-thumbnail)

**A great thumbnail is the face of your artwork. Capture creative and engaging thumbnails to attract viewers.**

![selectaspectratio](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FSelect%20Aspect%20Ratio.87dc9756.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Select Aspect Ratio[](#select-aspect-ratio)

Select the aspect ratio for your thumbnail in the Gallery. You can choose from landscape, square, or portrait. Landscape suits wide objects like stylish sports cars or futuristic spaceships. Portrait suits tall objects like towering skyscrapers or flowers. Square works well for front-facing objects or top views. Choose the aspect ratio that best fits your work.

![togglethirdgrid](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FToggle%20Third%20Grid.fe6cfb64.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Toggle Third Grid[](#toggle-third-grid)

For professional framing, activate the 3x3 grid to capture a balanced thumbnail image. Placing your object at one of the grid intersections creates visual balance, emphasizes your subject, and results in a stable composition.

![capturethumbnail](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2FCapture%20Thumbnail.1ff5bbbd.jpeg&w=3840&q=75)

### Capture Thumbnail[](#capture-thumbnail-1)

Tap the capture button to set the thumbnail image. The thumbnail will appear alongside other artworks in the Gallery.

# Blender for Feather

![blenderforfeather](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fblenderforfeather.d8f923fb.jpg&w=3840&q=75)

'Blender for Feather' helps you handle artworks created in Feather more conveniently within Blender.

You can find add-on in the right sidebar of the Blender viewport panel. If the sidebar is not visible, press 'N' on your keyboard or click the arrow icon in the top right corner.

Supports Blender versions 3.0 or higher (verified up to version 4.2.0 LTS).

**Note** 
'Blender for Feather' only works correctly when you imported feather artworks by OBJ file.

**Note** 
The file you need to download differs for Blender 3.x and 4.x versions.

### Join Curves[](#join-curves)

Joins curves into a single object based on criteria.

- Group : Join curves by group.

- Material : Join curves by their color.

- All : Join curves into one object.

### Decimate Curves[](#decimate-curves)

Optimizes the selected curves for better performance.

- Collapse: Optimizes by reducing the number of unnecessary vertices.

- Planar: Optimizes by reducing faces that do not form enough angles with each other.

**Tips** 
Collpase is useful in most cases, while planar is useful for curves drawn flat.

### Remesh Curves[](#remesh-curves)

Converts the selected curves into a manifold mesh.

- Voxel size : Sets the unit size for remeshing. Lower values result in higher detail but take more time.

**Tips** 
Remeshing makes artworks created in Feather easier to print with a 3D printer.

### Correct Colors[](#correct-colors)

Adjusts the colors of the selected curves.

- Brighten: Lightens the colors of the selected curves.

- Darken: Darkens the colors of the selected curves.

**Tips** 
Use this if the colors appear differently between Blender and Feather.

### Change Alpha Mode[](#change-alpha-mode)

Changes the transparency rendering method of the selected curves. Each click toggles between the alpha blend and alpha hashed modes.

Alpha blend provides a cleaner appearance but may not render transparency as expected. Alpha hashed renders transparency as expected but can introduce noise.

### Switch Diffuse Color and Emission Color[](#switch-diffuse-color-and-emission-color)

The first click removes the shading by setting the emission instead of diffuse for the selected curves. Clicking again restores the shading to its original state.

**Tips** 
Click once to view the curves in the same way as seen in Feather.

### Extract Curves[](#extract-curves)

Extracts the centerline of the curve geometry.

**Tips** 
This feature is useful for integrating Feather with other Blender features like Grease Pencil and Geometry Nodes.

**Note** 
Extract Curves does not function properly if decimate or remesh has been applied.

# Multi Material Master

![multimaterialmaster](/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fmultimaterialmaster.e614d709.jpg&w=3840&q=75)

'Multi Material Master' helps you to control multiple materials at once.

You can find add-on in the right sidebar of the Blender viewport panel. If the sidebar is not visible, press 'N' on your keyboard or click the arrow icon in the top right corner.

Supports Blender versions 3.0 or higher (verified up to version 4.2.0 LTS).

**Note** 
The file you need to download differs for Blender 3.x and 4.x versions.

### Generate Group Node Input[](#generate-group-node-input)

Adds group nodes to the inputs of the principled BSDF in the materials of the selected objects. By manipulating these group nodes, you can control all materials at once. For example, this allows you to simultaneously adjust the saturation, roughness, and other properties of all materials.

### Bake Colors to Vertex Colors[](#bake-colors-to-vertex-colors)

Store the color (base color or emission color) in the vertex color of the selected objects.

**Note** 
This is useful in cases where there is a limitation on the number of materials, such as with Sketchfab.
