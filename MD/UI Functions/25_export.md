# Extensions Export

Learn how to export your sketches in different formats.
## Export Tab
Quickly export your sketches to various platforms. Supports image, video, and 3D model
exports.
## Tap Export in System Menu
Tap export in the system menu at the top right of the screen to open the Export Tab.

## Quick Export
Quickly export using preset values. You can quick export in PNG, GIF, and GLTF formats. GIFs are
rendered as 360-degree turntable animations centered on the current orbit point.
## Format
Export in more formats with detailed settings.

## Image Export
Export as PNG or JPG. You can select the aspect ratio and size of the image. If you export as PNG,
a transparent background option is available. When the transparent background is toggled,
effects are not applied.
Learn more about Effects.

## Video Export
Export as MP4 or GIF. When exporting as MP4, you can adjust the bitrate and frame rate.

## MP4 Export
Tap 'Start Recording' and then tap the record button located at the bottom center of the screen.
During recording, you can navigate freely and pause or cancel at any time. Tap the record button
once again to stop the recording.

## Preset
Tap the preset list to change the video preset. You can choose from Sequence, 360 Rotation, and
Timelapse.
## Sequence
Exports the sequence as an MP4. A sequence must be set up before exporting.
Learn more about Sequences.
## 360 Rotation
Exports a 360-degree turntable animation. It follows the current orbit point.
Learn more about Orbit Points.

## Timelapse
Exports a timelapse animation. The timelapse follows the group order, and you can navigate freely
during recording.
## GIF Export
GIF Export via Video Export exports the 360 turntable animation just like a Quick Export centered
on the current orbit point.

## 3D Model Export
Export as OBJ or GLTF format. Only groups with visibility turned ON will be exported. If you want
to export only specific parts of your artwork or sketch, hide the other groups. The GLTF format
exports with baked vertex painting. For editing the exported OBJ or GLTF models in Blender, refer
to Blender Add-on.
