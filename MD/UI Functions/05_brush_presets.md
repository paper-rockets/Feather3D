# Brushes Brush Preset

Learn how to add and load brush presets.
## Save Brush Preset and Load
Save your brush size, color, and shape to create your own presets. Load them anytime to
continue working quickly.
## Open and Close Brush Preset

Tap the small arrow icon above the Brush Panel on the left side of the screen to open the brush
preset menu. Tap the arrow again to close the preset menu. Even when closed, added brush
presets remain saved. Brush presets are saved per note.
## Add Brush Preset
Tap Add brush preset or the + icon at the top to add a brush preset. The brush preset saves the
current brush type, color, size, and opacity. You can save multiple brush presets.

## Load
Tap a saved brush preset to load its settings. Tap and slide up or down in the brush preset list to
view more presets.

## Select and Deselect
Tap and hold a brush preset to select it. To select multiple brush presets, tap other presets while
one is already selected. To deselect, tap a selected brush preset again.

## Delete
While a brush preset is selected, tap the trash can icon to delete it. Deleted brush presets cannot
be recovered and will not return even if you undo the action.
