# Selection Duplicate

Learn how to duplicate selected sketches.
## Duplicate
Duplicate is essential for repetitive patterns, structural shapes, or just faster sketching.
## Select Curve and Tap Duplicate
Select the curve you want to duplicate and tap the leftmost icon in the duplicate at the bottom of
the screen. A message will appear with the number of duplicated curves. The duplicated curves

are in the same position as the original, so be careful not to confuse them.
## Duplicate Symmetrically
Symmetry and asymmetry play important roles in both nature and human society, but
people tend to recognize symmetry more and see it as a symbol of beauty and order. Use
Duplicate Symmetrically.
## Symmetrically by View
Tap the icon to the right of Duplicate to duplicate symmetrically based on the view direction. If the
sketch is skewed to the right, it will be duplicated to the left, and vice versa. This is useful for
quickly creating symmetrical sketches.

## Symmetrically by Mirror
Symmetrically by Mirror can only be used when the mirror is on. It duplicates symmetrically based
on the currently active mirror axis. If multiple axes are active, multiple curves will be duplicated at
once.
