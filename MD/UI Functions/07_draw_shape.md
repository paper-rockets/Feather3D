# Assistance Draw Shape

Learn about Draw Shape, useful for drawing straight lines, curved lines, arcs, and circles.
## Switch to Draw Shape
Tap the pencil icon in the top right tool menu to switch to Draw Shape. When the icon changes to
a pencil with a ruler beneath it, you're in Draw Shape mode. You can do everything in Draw Shape
that you can in Draw mode, such as drawing curves or creating 3D Guides.

## Draw Straight Lines and Curved Lines
In Draw Shape mode, when you draw a curve, it will automatically be corrected to a straight line
or a smooth curve based on how close your drawn curve is to those shapes. Similarly, if you draw
a circle, it will be corrected to a perfect circle.

## Hold to Adjust Straight Lines
Draw a straight line and keep the pen pressed against the screen. While holding, you can adjust
the length and endpoint of the line. Lift the pen to finalize the straight line.

## Hold to Adjust Curved Lines
Draw a curved line and keep the pen pressed against the screen. While holding, you can adjust
the curvature. Lift the pen to finalize the adjusted curved line.

## Create Circles
Press and hold the pen on the screen, then drag to create a circle. The size of the circle is
determined by how much you drag. Lift the pen to finalize the circle.

## Draw 3D Guides with Draw Shape
Use Draw Shape not only for curves but also to create 3D Guides. This also includes the ability to
Bend 3D Guides.
